📚 Environmental Facts

🌳

Trees need care

Planting is only the beginning. Suitable species, healthy soil, enough water, and ongoing care help young trees survive.

💧

Save water

Repairing leaks, turning off taps when not needed, and using water thoughtfully can reduce unnecessary waste.

♻️

Reduce waste

Avoiding unnecessary purchases and reusing items can reduce the resources needed to make new products.

🦋

Protect biodiversity

Native plants can provide food and shelter for local wildlife. Choose planting projects suited to the local ecosystem.

🌳 Tree Explorer: Meet Our Green Neighbours

Tap into the benefits of different trees. Benefits depend on local conditions and the species planted.

🌿
Everyday uses

Neem

Neem is valued in India for shade and several traditional uses. Its flowers and other parts can also be part of local food webs.

Care tip: Choose a suitable sunny location and allow enough room for its mature size.
🌳
Wildlife habitat

Banyan

Its broad canopy and spreading structure can offer shelter and nesting places for birds and other wildlife.

Care tip: Banyans need plenty of space; they are not suitable for every small garden.
🍃
Shade and habitat

Peepal

Peepal trees can provide shade, support wildlife and contribute to the structure of urban and rural green spaces.

Care tip: Plant away from foundations, pipes and narrow pavements.
🥭
Food and shade

Mango

Mango trees provide fruit and shade; flowers and fruit can be resources for some insects, birds and other animals.

Care tip: Check local climate, space and water needs before planting.
🌲
Forest ecosystems

Teak

Teak is a valuable timber tree. Responsible forest management helps reduce pressure on forests and habitats.

Care tip: Choose species recommended for your region rather than planting a tree just because it is popular.
🌼
Local biodiversity

Native flowering trees

Suitable native species can provide nectar, fruit, nesting opportunities and shelter for wildlife in the local ecosystem.

Care tip: Ask a local nursery, botanist or forest department which species suit your area.
🌎 Why trees matter to people: Trees store carbon as they grow, provide shade, help reduce some local heat, protect soil in suitable locations, support the water cycle and create habitats. Their benefits depend on species, climate, location and care.
